# Demo recording

The budget walkthrough is a narrated sequence of captured application screens. Its first scenes show the fictional example. Its final scenes show the team's public testnet round and successful receipt verification. All public testnet identities and the registry authority are team controlled.

The earlier single-payment walkthrough remains in public/payment-walkthrough.mp4. It uses a historical independent DAO payment for its final scene.

The source code and the public replay are the reproducible record of behavior.
